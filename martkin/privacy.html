Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 (version 1.0)

This policy explains how the Martkin game for iOS and Android (the "App") handles data. The App has no accounts, sign-up or login; we never ask for your name, email or phone number.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (store, city, money, level, upgrades, daily missions, weekly league score, sound and language settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the App deletes this data. The rival stores in the weekly league are AI; your score is not sent to other players.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads, shown through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device:

  • Advertising identifier (iOS IDFA – only if you allow it; Android advertising ID and app set ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (app launches, taps, video views)
  • Diagnostics (app and SDK performance)

This data is processed by Google and encrypted in transit. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Watching rewarded ads (2x income, fast shoes, gifts, multiplying offline earnings, etc.) is entirely optional.

3. Usage statistics

To improve the game we use Google Firebase Analytics: in-game events such as app opens, session length, store started, builds and upgrades, mission and login rewards claimed, and rewarded ads watched; device model, operating system, language, country; app instance ID and (if allowed) advertising identifier. This data is processed by Google. Google Privacy Policy.

We do not identify you by name with this data, and we collect no account or contact details.

4. Consent and your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: The App asks for your consent to personalised ads through Google's consent management platform (UMP). If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • iOS: The App asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared. You can change this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Ad content is limited to be suitable for a general audience.

6. Selling and sharing data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share data with third parties other than the Google services described above.

7. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies, and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to access your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing and lodge a complaint. Apart from the game progress on your device, we hold no data that identifies you. For ad data you can use Google's tools or contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version, with its date, is always on this page. If a new data-collecting service is added to the App, this page is updated beforehand.
